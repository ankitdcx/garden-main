# Historical Garden Chat Upgrade Audit — 2026-09-14

Status: **AUDIT / NON-CANONICAL / DOES NOT MODIFY v15.5**

## Purpose and retrieval boundary

This audit was requested to recover worthy Garden upgrades scattered across old project chats without loading the entire history into one context. Historical material was retrieved in bounded chronological/thematic passes, each pass was evaluated against the current v15.5 five-file source, duplicates were discarded, and only distinct surviving candidates were preserved.

The available personal-context retrieval system is semantic rather than a literal export/list of every chat. Therefore this audit cannot honestly claim byte-for-byte inspection of every historical message. It does cover the major retrievable Garden discussion lineage from early theory work through April/May governance and safety, June reliability/physical hardening, July GSL/agency/recursive assurance, August epistemic/federation/theory/domain work, and September CPI/CLIC/GCL/reasoning/KR proposals. No-match retrievals were not treated as evidence that a conversation never existed.

## Audit rule

For each recovered idea:

1. compare against current v15.5 whole-source semantics;
2. prefer existing owners and NO_CHANGE where current Garden already covers the requirement;
3. distinguish source-design semantics from implementation/conformance work;
4. preserve explicit deferred candidates instead of silently dropping them;
5. do not resurrect previously rejected ideas without new evidence;
6. do not mutate published v15.5;
7. require ordinary multi-model/GSL/human promotion before successor admission.
