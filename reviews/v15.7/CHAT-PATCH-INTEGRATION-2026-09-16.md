## Existing concepts reaffirmed rather than duplicated

### Current-best / final semantics
No new object is required. "Final", "complete" and "no known gaps" in iterative Garden work mean current best under the stated evidence/scope and do not block later challenge, supersession or correction.

### Explain
Explain already exists. The Human-Effect Closure patch adds a specific test: emergent multi-agent shorthand may be efficient, but consequential semantics must remain recoverable into canonical human-auditable meaning.

### Reputation
The existing domain-specific reputation vector remains authoritative for design. The log-scale proposal is only an optional projection and cannot replace the underlying evidence vector.

### Justice System
No new top-level justice engine is introduced. Anti-obstruction and evidence-continuity behavior attach to existing Justice/Evidence/Audit/Authority owners.

## Explicitly excluded from the public design repository

- private allegations about identifiable individuals;
- private personal complaint details;
- unverified claims presented as established facts;
- demographic/ethnic/religious collective-guilt mappings;
- operational instructions to disable, seize, sabotage or defeat real weapons;
- any claim that AI capability itself creates political authority.

These exclusions preserve Garden's own evidence, privacy, rights, authority and human-sovereignty rules.
