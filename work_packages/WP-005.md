# WP-005 — Public swarm candidate intake and cross-reference

Status: OPEN

## Objective

Admit public `garden-swarm` findings into the private Garden integration process only as provenance-preserved candidates. Verify every claimed gap against the complete Garden v15.5 five-file source before proposing a successor patch.

## Required process

1. Freeze the public review/run receipt, source hashes, target identity, reviewer model-family identities, independent findings, peer cross-examination dispositions, and candidate synthesis.
2. Require a valid `GardenDesignReviewBundle/v1` for semantic/design candidates: at least three distinct qualified reviewer families on the same bounded target, all independent findings completed before peer cross-examination, at least three independent final dispositions, and explicit zero-cost evidence for every successful external reviewer call. Prefer four reviewer families when available.
3. Reject malformed, stale, provenance-incomplete, self-admitting, non-free/non-zero-cost, or insufficiently reviewed bundles. `INSUFFICIENT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW` and `INSUFFICIENT_PEER_CROSS_EXAMINATION` admit no semantic delta.
4. Bind the candidate evidence to the current DesignEpoch and complete five-file source root. Public/non-ZDR reviewers receive only already-public Garden material.
5. Search the complete five-file canonical source for every affected anchor/term and evidence request.
6. Classify each candidate as ALREADY_COVERED, DEFECT_CONFIRMED, IMPROVEMENT_CANDIDATE, NEEDS_EVIDENCE, or REJECTED.
7. For confirmed defects/improvements, produce affected invariants/contracts/tests and compare against DO_NOTHING.
8. Run applicable Compare/Reason/Proof/Evidence separation, AAP, algebra/process, authority, ActionGate, semantic/formal/property/security/integration checks, preserving explicit UNKNOWN/FRONTIER.
9. Preserve dissent/blockers; model agreement is evidence, never proof or authority.
10. Only a separately governed scoped patch may enter the DesignEpoch-bound successor-delta manifest. Public review evidence never modifies `canonical/current/`, mints authority, or promotes canon.

## Machine intake boundary

`scripts/validate_design_review_bundle.py` performs the first private fail-closed intake check. A PASS means only `candidate_evidence_eligible=true` for whole-five-file cross-reference. It explicitly leaves authorization and semantic admission unevaluated.

## Initial source

Public PR: `ankitdcx/garden-swarm#25` and its bounded heterogeneous swarm receipts; subsequent public findings are governed by the persistent Design Review Matrix.

Public swarm/reviewer output has no automatic authority over `canonical/current/`.
