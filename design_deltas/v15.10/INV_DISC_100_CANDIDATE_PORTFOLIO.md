## Evaluation gates

1. Prior art: query both motif/technique and domain application; retain dated query log and near-duplicate papers.
2. Formalize equations, safety sets, measurable outcome, and falsifier. Reject analogy-only entries.
3. Compare against the strongest available specialist model, a generic-search arm, and a Garden-guided arm with equal data and computational budget.
4. Freeze held-out data, endpoints, stopping conditions, candidate denominator, and independent checker before test.
5. Publish null results, rejected candidates, code, assumptions, source provenance, and uncertainty.
6. Never label NOVEL or USEFUL without independent prior-art review and held-out value evidence.

## Research strategy

Prioritize *novelty-screen survivors*, not generator self-ratings. Existing strong methods (SOS/SMT/CEGIS, state estimation, MPC and domain-specific physics) are the baselines. Structured-system and interface-invariant searches are plausible hypotheses for improvement, not established gaps.

## Related control

The companion INV-DISC-001 symbolic control suite generated 100 parameterized two-dimensional nonlinear systems: 99 satisfied a conservative analytic sufficient condition for ellipse forward invariance; one was INCONCLUSIVE under that condition. This is not a Garden-vs-baseline trial, a novelty screen, or a physical experiment.
