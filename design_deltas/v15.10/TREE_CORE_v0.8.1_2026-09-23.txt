GARDEN 15.x — CORE TREE
Structural integration candidate v0.8.1 | 23 September 2026

PURPOSE AND STATUS

This is the working structure for rebuilding Garden as one coherent design.
It gives existing rules and proposed upgrades a place to attach; it does not
rewrite their full content. The old five-file and proposed three-source
packages are inputs to the rebuild, not a required file layout for the result.
The target input set includes the exact v15.9.1 suite, separately identified
v15.10 candidates, and other additions. Preserve each item's original
current/candidate/research and qualification status. A tree heading never
admits a candidate or changes a rule.
The attached GARDEN_TECHNICAL_proposed_v15.10.txt is an additional source
candidate, explicitly WORKING CANDIDATE_NOT_CANONICALLY_ADMITTED.
Five newly attached v15.9.1 text files are an EARLIER BYTE-PREFIX EDITION
of the manifest-verified v15.9.1 COMPLETE archive; see source binding below.

This is a structural candidate, not a new canonical Garden release, a
completed atomic register, or a GROUP_REVIEW PASS. The tree is ready to guide
the coherent rewrite now. Unit-by-unit retention checks come before a claim
that the rewrite includes everything.

REBUILD RULE

Keep the meaning and history of every material old part; do not require every
old part to remain a separate active part. Duplicates may merge, rules may be
superseded by a governed decision, and real conflicts remain visible. Every
outcome is recorded. The new design must eventually stand on its own without
asking readers to piece together the old files.

THE TREE AND ITS CROSS-CUTTING VIEWS

  0 ROOT          Purpose; binding rights, authority and other hard limits
    |
  1 SUBSTRATE     Typed meaning, qualified identity and source projections
    |
  2 TRUNK         Common control, knowledge, checks and change mechanisms
    |
  3 BOUNDARY      Contracts for theories, profiles, institutions, tools and
                  implementations that grow outward

  R REGISTER      Exact source-to-owner records and historic dispositions;
                  supports every level, not a new source of authority
  G RELEASE GATE  Two-way retention and independent review before any claim
                  that the new design preserves its declared predecessors

One semantic assertion has one versioned, scoped controlling owner in the
resulting design. Record its former owner as history; assigning a new owner
requires a qualified migration outcome. The tree is a readable home, not the
whole architecture. The same qualified contracts also yield:
  - an effect/control view: applicable gates, times, order and fallback;
  - a P0–P11 lifecycle view: governed transitions and their owners;
  - a capability/dependency view: what supplies what and when it activates;
  - a historical view: old source -> disposition -> new contract.
Cross-branch links describe dependencies without adding Core Relations,
extra parents or permission. A generated view is not controlling by itself.

0. ROOT — THE THIN CONSTITUTIONAL BOUNDARY

  0.1 Purpose, beneficiaries, scope and applicable nonhuman considerations.
  0.2 Constitutional precedence and the rights registry, with exact status.
  0.3 Human sovereignty, consent, relational influence and Human-Effect Closure.
  0.4 Legitimate authority, delegation, revocation and non-self-authorization.
  0.5 Safety, K0P, grounded emergency admission, privacy and applicable law.
  0.6 Treatment of unknown, conflicting, incomplete and stale claims.
  0.7 Constitutional and major-architecture change governance.

These are routing slots for existing controlling obligations, not abbreviated
replacements for them. The inspected v15.10 text Catalogue marks RIGHT-001
through RIGHT-025 active, RIGHT-026 reserved and RIGHT-027 on hold. Preserve
the actual source wording, applicability, exceptions and status. No schema,
model, credential, consensus, generated receipt or effective action gives
itself authority. Constitution > Law > Policy > Decision/Optimization in the
inspected text edition, with jurisdiction and equal-rank conflict handled by
their controlling contracts. Consent, safety, privacy, rights and proof gates
can remain independently applicable. A high-level root heading cannot
silently make a domain rule universal or promote a candidate to current.

2. TRUNK — COMMON CONTROL AND ASSURANCE

Put a contract in Trunk when different kinds of modules can call the same
shared behavior without importing a particular domain's law or theory. Give
that shared behavior typed inputs, outputs, owner, scope, failure and stopping
rules. Keep a theory's equations or a domain's implementation with its own
module even when it offers a Trunk interface. Trunk placement does not make
that function always active or grant it independent authority. The source's
activation classes still decide when it runs.

  2.1 Observe/Type/Compare/Reason/Candidates/Select/Guard/Act/Observe/Correct-or-stop.
      This is a control-level pattern, not a requirement that every task run
      one serial procedure. COMPARE and REASON are Garden control operations;
      R0 SanityGate terminates without recursive Reason. Keep resource and
      stopping contracts, typed outcomes, AAP and FunctionContracts sourced.
      The exact v15.9.1 User "UNIVERSAL MEANINGFUL STEP" and System
      "Universal TransitionStep loop" put the final Guard AFTER selecting
      a candidate and BEFORE any effect. A cheap filter can discard bad
      candidates before selection; it is not the final effect guard.
      The separate v15.10 text Technical §9 says Guard -> Select. Preserve
      that as a source-qualified ordering discrepancy, not an override of
      the predecessor's corrected selected-candidate guard.
      v15.9.1 also keeps Verity/Proof V0–V4 and Safety S0–S4 tiers and three
      activation classes (universal kernel, context-triggered cross-cutting,
      domain/execution) with their actual owner contracts; this heading
      does not collapse them into one gate or one always-on runtime cost.

  2.2 Actual-effect gate product.
      At selection, dispatch, and every continuing-effect boundary, determine
      which sourced gates apply and re-evaluate those that can change. Each
      gate record identifies: controlling owner; scope; validity/freshness;
      check time; result type; ordering/dependencies; source-specific
      fallback. Candidate gate families include action authority and its
      revocation witness, consent, human effects, purpose/privacy, rights,
      K0P, independently grounded FET hazard, applicable law, proof and
      domain safety. This list is a discovery checklist, not an invented
      universal hard-gate order. The attached Technical defines a six-axis
      GardenApplicabilityAssessment and LiveAuthorizationWitness bound to a
      decision nonce, effect commitment, authority/revocation/time snapshots
      and an owner-qualified atomic linearization or bounded lease. Its
      structural result still requires owner authority evaluation; a digest
      cannot authenticate the witness or create authority. Bind actual
      fallbacks and refresh rules from each applicable owner. Late consent
      revocation must invalidate the act even if another gate remains valid.

  2.3 Knowledge, evidence, comparison, reasoning and proof.
      KR, belief timelines and epistemic transitions, historical belief
      reconstruction, representation coverage, causal claims and independent
      verification keep separate owners/status. No agreement from shared
      representation alone counts as independent confirmation. Learning,
      DesignMirror, OntologicalDebtView, adversarial future stress and
      GlobalInvariantConsistencyReceipt attach to their existing owners.

  2.4 Process, policy, decision, conformance and evidence algebras.
      Preserve each operator's operands, laws, NON-LAWS, effect ordering,
      conflict behavior and composition bridge. The per-step loop is one
      application of Process Algebra. CLIC's five operations and GCL's typed
      counterfactual arithmetic attach here with their actual v15.9.1 owner
      spans; neither creates authority or a new fundamental force.

  2.5 Generative closure and change qualification.
      Route v15.9.1 [H/S/T/A-GCSC-1591] and the v15.10 GCSC patch to their
      original owners: GCSC bounded L0-L5 search, SAL's distinct structural,
      completeness, join, coherence and interpretation statuses, materiality
      oracles/RAW denominator, and SAC candidate artifacts/tests/receipts.
      Preserve generated-candidate status and resource-bound INCOMPLETE.
      CMUR, CPI, RCI/RCC, CDDT, OCF, MSC, TML and recursive/epoch change
      have separate source-qualified owners and admission gates. RCI is a
      reading of control, never a new engine or grant of authority.

  2.6 Shared theory interface and composition checks.
      SRT, VSM, RPM, PPM, TFT, FET and SCT can expose common
      TheoryEquationContract interfaces: types, units, domains, assumptions,
      bounds, validity, falsifiers, adapters and qualification. Their actual
      equations and specialist rules belong to their separately qualified
      Boundary modules. Composition depends on those contracts and domain
      science. UPS and other speculative theories keep RESEARCH status.
      This index does not replace the full Theories source.

  2.7 Lifecycle, distributed coordination and recovery.
      P0–P11 are governed lifecycle views, not compulsory serial functions.
      Multi-agent independence, institutional incentives and protected
      reporting, physical action, monitoring, correction, stopping, resource
      bounds and transition continuity attach under their true owners.

3. BOUNDARY — HOW THE REST OF THE DESIGN GROWS

A Boundary module is a real part of the design, not a heading or storage bin.
Its contract states: purpose; qualified owner, version and status; conditions
for activation; typed inputs and outputs; dependencies and versions; applicable
Root limits, facets and live gates; failures, freshness, invalidators and
fallback; equations, assumptions and non-laws where relevant; tests and
qualification. A field that does not apply is explained, not silently dropped.
If structured interpretation is not admitted, keep SOURCE_FALLBACK_REQUIRED.
Current meaning must be stated in the resulting controlling design; an old
archive locator alone cannot supply a missing current chapter.

An item can have one home and several typed links: requires a shared interface,
provides that interface, is constrained by a rule, is invalidated by an event,
or is historically derived from a predecessor. These are architecture links,
NOT new members of the 24 Garden Core Relations. A link cannot confer
permission, proof or authority.

Place the following as explicitly named OUTWARD INTAKE QUEUES, never as a
claim that their atomic content has been migrated:

  - seven theory bodies; scoped theories, adapters and qualified profiles;
    TED/SEA/MAC/NRS/VIP/SHE/EC/CSR/AR/MI specializations;
  - reference architecture presets TRAIN, GAIA Titanium/Diamond, GSL-KR,
    GSL-AR, GSL-R, GESTALT/APAO and Reliability OS;
  - governance and institutional UBC/TSI/TIA/ART/IIC/PIR/CSI/SMCD/CAD/OAC/
    CTSN/PAI, HSA, HRAP and relational influence;
  - v15.1–v15.9.1 additions including CLIC, GCL, TRACE, EDCR, engineering
    hardening, physical/cognitive closure and P001–P037;
  - law, security, privacy, standards, medicine, finance, research,
    deployment and other specialized application families;
  - Annexure's exact SchemaIDs, REG-*, invariant, TEST-* and artifact
    definitions, including aliases, reserved IDs and machine bindings;
  - v15.10 GCSC patch and v15.11 REP, RCC and other later proposals with
    statuses kept separate from predecessor current meaning. Exact local
    REP and RCC file hashes are known; atomic owner/effect bindings remain
    UNMAPPED. Other later candidates require source enumeration.

An item may touch several queues but has one qualified controlling owner.
The queues are a search checklist. Every actual item, including exceptions,
equations and tests, requires a real record before it can count as retained.

R. ATOMIC REGISTER AND CROSSWALK — REQUIRED SUPPORT STRUCTURE

Required per-record fields:
  stable qualified ID; exact source edition and SHA-256; exact anchor/span
  and span hash; old owner where applicable and new controlling owner;
  content type; current meaning or exact controlling text; scope and
  effective period;
  design/theory/proof/empirical/application/freshness status axes; applicable
  facets; prerequisites and typed inbound/outbound edges; formulas, laws and
  non-laws; exceptions and invalidators; effect-time gates; tests/receipts;
  predecessor and successor identity; and disposition with reason.

Hash rule: source_sha256 is SHA-256 of the exact supplied file bytes (or
the named ZIP member bytes), with no newline, Unicode, BOM or whitespace
normalization. A source_span_sha256 hashes the exact byte slice in that
identified file using a declared half-open [start_byte,end_byte) range.
Human line/anchor locators are supplementary; when selecting by line,
derive byte offsets in that exact file and record them. Normalized-text
comparison may aid review but must not silently merge two source editions.

Disposition values: PRESERVED, GENERALIZED_WITH_PROOF, MERGED_WITH_EXACT
REMAINDER, SUPERSEDED_WITH_GOVERNED_PROOF, REJECTED, DEFERRED, RESEARCH,
CONFLICT, UNMAPPED. A duplicate may merge only with its exact unique
remainder preserved. A superseded rule remains visible as history; a rejected
candidate is not erased. UNMAPPED or CONFLICT blocks a lossless release claim
but does not block work on the architecture. Maintain both directions:
  exact old anchor -> qualified current owner -> generated tree/view;
  every new controlling claim -> exact source or explicitly admitted change.
Do not use a bare "Technical §17" without an edition-qualified filename and
hash: §17 is real in the inspected v15.10 text Technical, but that citation
does not locate the differently numbered v15.9.1 Technical source.

G. NO-LOSS AND ADMISSION GATE — NOT A TREE BRANCH

The architecture and representative modules can be drafted while the register
is incomplete. Before claiming a completed, lossless integrated release,
freeze the exact v15.9.1 source suite and its GCSC closure records, the
selected v15.10 source package(s), GCSC patch, and all proposed additions
actually in scope, together with their
hashes/statuses. Compare every material unit in both directions. Check IDs,
source spans, equations, operator order, non-laws, applicable gates and
effect timing, negative/UNKNOWN cases, historical aliases, supersession and
document ownership. Set predecessor sources aside and test whether the
resulting current sources alone define all current meaning. Run owner-sized
blind critique, reconcile minority counterexamples, re-review material
changes, and verify independently. External reviewer quorum and semantic
admission remain separately recorded. A build PASS does not imply those
later states.
