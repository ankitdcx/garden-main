# GARDEN-IDTD-001 — Invariant-Driven Theory Discovery

**Status:** NONCANONICAL ADDITIVE CANDIDATE; not implemented, validated, certified, or admitted.  
**Target:** v15.10 generative abstraction (GCSC / SAL / Materiality / SAC).  
**Compatible:** v15.11 RCC, REP, VEI, and recovered EVO/SEM directions.  
**Canonical effect:** NONE. No new top-level Form, engine, authority, or self-admission route.

## Purpose

Discover candidate theories and missing mechanisms by reversing the usual problem-first design flow:

`Irreducible seed + seed-justified fundamental invariants → GCSC situations → SAL admissibility → Materiality/Human-Effect → SAC obligations/artifacts → explanatory pattern search → candidate theory → independent falsification → existing-owner comparison → candidate upgrade.`

SAC derives engineering obligations (invariants, schemas, FunctionContracts, states, tests, proof obligations, dependencies, invalidators, audit and recovery). IDTD searches for a **general explanatory mechanism** behind several consequences, or a mechanism yielding a distinct falsifiable prediction. It reuses existing Garden machinery rather than becoming a parallel owner.

## Inputs and boundary

Each run freezes SeedVersion, constitutional/primitive meanings, protected properties, source invariant IDs and derivations, registries, GCSC/SAL/Materiality/SAC profiles, domain inputs, permitted representations, search limits, source corpus/index snapshot and verifier independence profile. Unjustified invariants cannot be silently promoted to seeds.

No new normative right, value, consent, permission, authority, factual claim, empirical parameter or exception may be invented to make a theory work. Such gaps are reported as UNKNOWN or as explicit proposed seed changes requiring separate authority and admission.

Materiality and Human-Effect checks remain active during pattern discovery; neither low-frequency novelty nor multiple locally compliant steps can erase applicable obligations. Out-of-profile and resource-exhausted results remain unresolved.

## Procedure

1. **Freeze and enumerate.** Select a bounded, stratified set of seed-justified invariants and SAL-admissible situations; retain graph/topology, context, uncertainty and search denominators.
2. **Derive.** Run Materiality and SAC; preserve all obligations, non-PASS states, evidence dependencies, exceptions and original derivation paths.
3. **Find patterns.** Cluster consequences by shared causal/structural mechanism, not name or embedding similarity alone. Seek minimal explanatory hypotheses and alternative representations.
4. **Construct theory candidates.** Require a scoped mechanism with assumptions, typed inputs, predicted consequences, falsifiers, and at least one counterexample search. A candidate must explain multiple material consequences **or** make a distinct independently testable prediction beyond existing Garden.
5. **Compare ownership.** Check canonical source, v15.10 GSK-H1..H4, RCC/REP/VEI and all v15.11 candidates, plus recovered EVO/SEM directions. Classify FULL_EXISTING, PARTIAL_EXTENSION, NEW_CANDIDATE, CONTRADICTION or UNKNOWN. Reuse existing owners wherever possible.
6. **Attack.** Test counterexamples, seed invention, representation convergence, dependence, scope leakage, false positives, exception loss, over/under-materiality, and downstream human effects. A proposer may not certify its own output.
7. **Package only survivors.** Produce candidate theory, derived obligations, schemas/contracts/tests/proof plan, owner mapping, unresolved frontier, and independent review requirements. New module creation is *not* automatic; it requires surviving theory validation and explicit owner/topology analysis.
8. **Retain and report.** Preserve frozen inputs, source references, hashes, evidence, failures and unknowns. Generated candidates remain noncanonical until independently admitted through existing procedures.

## Hard invariants

- **IDTD-001 No foundation invention:** derived candidates cannot silently supply their own missing normative or empirical premises.
- **IDTD-002 No duplicate ownership:** FULL existing coverage produces reuse, not a new module.
- **IDTD-003 No self-validation:** independent review must establish semantic correctness and evidential support.
- **IDTD-004 No false completeness:** bounded search exhaustion cannot be reported as universal discovery or proof.
- **IDTD-005 No automatic admission:** theory generation never creates authority, certification or permission.
- **IDTD-006 Materiality closure:** Human-Effect and other applicable obligations persist through clustering and generalization.
- **IDTD-007 Explanatory gain:** every new theory explains multiple material observations or makes a distinct testable prediction.
- **IDTD-008 Retention and invalidation:** predecessor meaning, exceptions, diagnostics, dependency invalidation and uncertainty remain addressable.

## Tests and measurements

**A. Blinded rediscovery:** hide RCC, REP, VEI and other selected theory mechanisms from the proposer while retaining only permitted seeds and source invariants. Freeze holdouts before generation. Measure reconstructed mechanisms, necessary/sufficient assumptions, exceptions, predictions, tests, false positives, misses and semantic equivalence using independent judges.

**B. Novel theory discovery:** use frozen out-of-corpus situations, separate representation portfolios and independent adversarial review. Count *validated* novel explanatory mechanisms, not generated names; report duplicate rate, invalid novelty, false-positive rate, missed counterexamples and practical utility.

**C. Regression:** test seed drift, malicious pattern clustering, scope inflation, unknown-to-PASS, common-representation blindness, self-admission and conflicts with rights/authority/consent. Preserve negative receipts and non-PASS status.

Success thresholds must be specified **before** evaluation for each declared profile; no post-hoc target selection. A successful run demonstrates only bounded evidence, not proof of exhaustive theory discovery.

## Relationship to retained candidates

- **GSK-H1..H4:** examples of higher-order invariant families IDTD may rediscover or generalize; no parallel owner.
- **RCC / REP / VEI:** existing theory/control candidates used for ownership matching, falsification and held-out reconstruction.
- **EVO / SEM recovered directions:** complementary candidate lineage, not replaced or admitted by IDTD.
- **v15.10 retention recovery:** no semantic item retired without demonstrated lossless reconstruction and independent admission.

**Bottom line:** compress existing design by finding its fundamental invariants; expand design by deriving and testing explanatory theories from those invariants. Both directions remain candidate research until independently validated.
