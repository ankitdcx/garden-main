## Screening and admission process

1. Deduplicate by underlying equation/constraint/method, not by domain label. Do not use raw row count as discovery count.
2. Identify applicability: actual physical model, relevant actuator/sensor/mode/delay and validity domain; mark N/A where absent.
3. Derive specific quantitative consequence and falsifier; do not call standard monitoring indices new invariants.
4. Search technique and application prior art with query logs, date, databases, and near-duplicate analysis.
5. Compare strong specialist baseline, generic AI/search, and explicit Garden/CGCD method with matched budgets and independent checker.
6. Retain all rejected/negative/inconclusive receipts. No novelty or economic claim without independent evidence.
