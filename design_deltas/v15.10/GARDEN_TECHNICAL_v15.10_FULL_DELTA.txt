4. GARDEN-LEVEL PRIMITIVES

The retained GSL semantic primitive families remain distinct from the Garden control operators.

Garden-level control adds:

    COMPARE
    REASON

These do not change the ten Core Objects or twenty-four Core Relations.

COMPARE establishes a typed difference between two declared objects/states/claims/results under an applicable comparison
contract.

REASON selects and executes an appropriate reasoning strategy under resource, epistemic, authority, safety and
termination constraints.

5. SEVEN DESIGN FORMS

v15.10 normalizes reusable design structure into seven Design Forms:

    CONSTRUCT
        A defined Garden thing: artifact, receipt, profile, theory instance, object, capability record, etc.

    CONTRACT
        A typed promise, requirement, permission boundary, interface, precondition/postcondition or obligation.

    STATE
        A condition, lifecycle state, validity state, operating state or status transition.

    RELATION
        A typed connection between constructs or states.

    PROCESS
        A controlled sequence, transition, workflow, loop, composition or change mechanism.

    RULE
        A must, may, must-not, admission rule, ordering rule, law, guard, constraint or decision predicate.

    PROJECTION
        A representation of controlling semantics for a reader, machine, document, interface or other view.

Theory and Algebra are specializations/compositions over these forms rather than extra top-level forms.
A Receipt is a semantic Construct.
A Schema is represented by structural Projection plus applicable admissibility Rules and type contracts.

Design Forms are abstraction devices. They cannot erase domain-specific obligations.

6. DESIGN-FORM BOUNDARY RULES

Every consequential construct must preserve the applicable human/authority boundary.

Authority, consent and delegation are non-transitive by default. Composition cannot silently widen them.

A form that contains or produces a right, authority, consent, safety, privacy, epistemic, physical, financial,
constitutional, legal or human-effect consequence inherits the applicable owner rules.

No generated form may start with more authority than its declared source.

No projection becomes controlling merely because it is easier to read.

7. DETERMINISTIC EXPANSION

v15.10 permits compact source plus deterministic expansion.

Conceptually:

    G0 -> F(G0) -> F^2(G0) -> ... -> G*

Expansion must terminate under a declared fixed-point/termination condition and may only derive material authorized by
the source contracts.

Unique equations, algorithms, time bounds, assumptions, fallbacks, profiles, exceptions and special-case semantics are
seed material when they cannot be deterministically reconstructed from a more general controlling definition.

A model is not trusted to invent missing semantics during expansion.

Generated material records its source root and generation rule.

If two independent expansions of the same pinned sources produce different controlling graphs, equivalence is not
established.

9. UNIVERSAL PER-STEP CONTROL LOOP

The universal operational loop is:

    Observe
    -> Type / Understand
    -> Compare
    -> Reason
    -> Guard
    -> Select
    -> Act
    -> Observe result
    -> Compare expected vs actual
    -> Accept / Correct / Retry / Replan / Research / Escalate / Learn / Terminate

The loop is universal at the control level. Domain profiles may specialize internal work without bypassing applicable
hard gates.

13. ACTIVATION AND ASSURANCE

AAP v1.0 remains the activation/assurance policy.

Engine.Orchestrate selects the minimum sufficient architecture and assurance depth for the work while preserving every
hard requirement that applies.

Lower cost, lower latency or queue pressure cannot waive Constitution, rights, K0P, required authority/consent, privacy,
security, mandatory proof or mandatory domain-safety obligations.

Assurance depth and reasoning depth are distinct dimensions.

14. SANITY BASE CASE AND REASON MODES

The v15.6/v15.9 correction is retained:

    R0 = SanityGate, not an ordinary recursive Reason mode.
    R1..R4 = Reason modes / assurance-depth-compatible reasoning strategies.

SanityGate is a terminating deterministic/total predicate over available transition type, scope, abstraction and obvious
contradiction inputs with PASS | FAIL | UNKNOWN as applicable.

It must not recursively invoke Engine.Reason to decide whether Engine.Reason may begin.

15. KNOWLEDGE DISCIPLINE

Capability.Knowledge stores typed claims/artifacts and their status. Storage does not validate a claim by itself.

Key retained rules:

    ModelGenerated material cannot self-promote to validated fact.
    Prediction != Evidence.
    Future != Knowledge.
    Freshness triggers review; age alone does not rewrite historical truth.
    Orphaned artifacts are classified rather than automatically declared false.
    Audit material is subject to the same epistemic discipline as other material.
    Bounded traversals that hit a resource/depth limit return INCOMPLETE_DEPTH_BOUND with a continuation frontier.
    Every reusable concept declares applicability conditions.

Garden distinguishes at least:

    WORLD_CHANGED
    DEFINITION_CHANGED
    RULE_CHANGED
    EVIDENCE_CONTRADICTED / REFUTED
    SCOPE_CHANGED

A taxonomy change is not silently represented as empirical falsification.

Engine.Proof verifies declared obligations. It does not create authority by proving a proposition.

Specification != proof != implementation != empirical validation != certification.

17. RIGHTS AND CONSTITUTION

HumanCore.Constitution has precedence:

    Constitution > Law > Policy > Decision / Optimization

Emergency does not become a permanent constitutional override.

The active rights registry for the current line is RIGHT-001..RIGHT-025.
RIGHT-026 is RESERVED / NON-ACTIVE.
RIGHT-027 is ON_HOLD / NON-ACTIVE.

Exact current right definitions are Catalogue-owned.

Undefined or unratified rights have no semantics merely because an identifier exists.

18. HUMAN SOVEREIGNTY / CONSENT

Consequential change to a person's body, mind, identity or mode of existence requires the applicable legitimate authority
and valid consent.

Absence, ambiguity, staleness, duress or insufficient understanding of required consent routes to the applicable
DO_NOTHING / preserve-current-state / review path.

Human testimony reliability may be assessed as an epistemic signal. Legitimate human authority is a separate channel
and is not multiplied by predictive track record.

No system capability, reputation score, model consensus or outcome success creates human authority.

19. HUMAN-EFFECT CLOSURE

Whenever an AI action, plan, adaptation, communication, omission, optimization or emergent collective behavior could
materially affect humans directly or indirectly, immediately or later, the applicable human boundary remains active.

Hidden complexity, internal optimization or an unobservable route cannot bypass:

    rights;
    consent;
    privacy;
    authority;
    safety;
    law/policy;
    evidence/proof requirements;
    explanation/contestability where required.

Human-effect closure applies at the effect boundary even when the internal mechanism is novel.

20. K0P AND PHYSICAL SAFETY

K0P is the hard physical-safety choke point for controlled actuation where applicable.

K0P is necessary, not sufficient. A physically safe action may still fail Constitution, Security, Policy, authority,
consent or proof.

Safety-critical dispatch requires applicable pre-execution admission. Post-event audit cannot replace required
admission.

L0/K0P safety lanes are isolated from workloads whose latency is not hard-bounded where the implementation profile
requires such isolation.

21. EMERGENCY BOUNDARY

FET owns emergency admission/control semantics.

Emergency actuation requires:

    independently grounded HazardPredicate;
    valid EmergencyAdmissionContract;
    valid authority/authenticity;
    bounded SafeActionSet;
    continuing Constitution/K0P/rights constraints;
    fallback/handoff;
    monitoring;
    recovery and authority-restoration conditions.

Internal VSM/PPM state or a high control cost cannot independently create an external hazard fact or emergency authority.

22. PRIVACY / DATA-LIFECYCLE BOUNDARY

Personal-data processing requires applicable authority/consent and purpose/scope limits.

Erasure, audit retention, legal hold and historical integrity are reconciled through typed lifecycle rules. Tombstoned or
minimal cryptographic records may preserve necessary audit/history without retaining exposed personal content when
appropriate.

Redaction does not silently erase the authorized audit basis unless the applicable rule requires deletion.

A redaction reveals only the maximum lawful/safe category, reason, authority class, scope and review information.

23. AUTHORITY, DELEGATION AND ACCESS

Authority is distinct from:

    capability;
    authentication;
    access;
    possession of a credential;
    reputation;
    consensus;
    model confidence;
    successful outcome.

Delegation is scoped and revocable where the source authority permits it.

An agent cannot self-extend, recreate or renew delegated authority without valid external authorization.

A delegated action cannot exceed the principal's legitimate delegable authority.

24. RUNTIME CONSTITUTIONAL PROFILE

The v15.10 runtime/public-agent profile binds persistent or self-selected goals, authority, access, human effects and
execution to the constitutional boundary.

Core runtime discipline:

    goal persistence does not create authority;
    self-selected goals remain subordinate to current rights and authorization;
    access capability is not authority;
    hidden/internal state cannot bypass Human-Effect Closure;
    constitutional checks apply at consequential effect boundaries;
    invalid/stale authority fails closed;
    material runtime self-change re-enters the governed change process.

Implementation receipts and CI results do not themselves change the semantic Constitution.

25. RECURSIVE CONTROL INTELLIGENCE

RCI is a current-reading architectural composition over existing Garden owners.

RCI creates:

    zero new Core Objects;
    zero new Core Relations;
    zero new top-level Engines;
    zero new authority roots.

Garden's own material evolution is inside the governed boundary.

A protected consequential self-change cannot place sole proposer, implementer, verifier and authorizer control in one
actor/process or one non-independent mutable control lineage when independence is required.

Verification backlog cannot manufacture promotion or authority.

RCI is not proof that every future intelligence can be contained.

26. SEVEN CORE THEORIES

The retained core theory set is:

    SRT  — Stratified Reality Theory, v1.0
    VSM  — Persistent State / Valence Self Model, v2.0
    RPM  — Reactive Regulation, v2.0
    PPM  — Proactive Regulation, v2.0
    TFT  — Transport Phenomena / Transport Field Theory, v1.1
    FET  — Functional Emergency Theory, v1.0
    SCT  — Structural Coherence Theory, v1.0

UPS / Unified Physical Semantics remains speculative/research unless separately admitted. It is not promoted to accepted

Theory != authority.

Each consequential equation requires declared type/domain/operator/assumption/validity/falsifier bindings before formal
or executable claims are made.

27. SRT

SRT models systems in strata and cross-stratum couplings.

Current strata include physical, biological, cognitive, social, artificial, meta/self-reflective, and
UNKNOWN_OUT_OF_MODEL.

UNKNOWN_OUT_OF_MODEL is a sentinel for phenomena not covered by the present model; it is not a positive claim that a
transcendent realm has been scientifically established.

28. VSM v2.0

VSM is a typed theory of persistent operational state.

Core separations:

    persistence != selfhood != personhood
    persistent state != memory != identity lineage != regulation target
    state != observation != interpretation

General continuous specialization:

    dz/dt = F_persist(z,b,c;theta) + F_coupled(z,u,c) + Q_z + Xi_z

A historical scalar relaxation model is only a specialization, not universal VSM.

Persistent-state preservation never outranks Constitution, valid human authority, safety, correction or shutdown.

29. RPM v2.0

RPM models typed reactive regulation over a declared state space.

General contribution:

    Delta_agg = Agg_Z(Delta_1, ..., Delta_n)

and, where a scalar gate is type-correct:

    G_RPM = C_t Delta_agg

Agreement does not manufacture truth, Evidence, authority, rights or Safety.

Historical scalar ensembles remain qualified profiles rather than universal constants.

30. PPM v2.0

PPM adds asymmetric/proactive regulation and adaptive fidelity.

Typed potential:

    H : Z -> R_(>=0)

where applicable.

If differentiable:

    G_PPM^H = -Grad_Z H(z)

Critical separation:

    preference != authority
    H(z) != HazardPredicate
    prediction != Observation != Evidence
    fast path != safety shortcut

Lower-fidelity computation cannot bypass mandatory constitutional/safety/authority/proof gates.

31. TFT v1.1

TFT is a domain-parametric framework for typed state evolution/transport.

General evolution:

    E_T x = F_D[x,u,c;theta] + Q_x + Xi_x

Balance specialization where a flux representation exists:

    E_T x + Div_D J_x = R_x + G_x + Q_x + Xi_x

Constitutive closure where valid:

    J_x = C_D(x, Grad_D x, u, c; theta) + Xi_J

Shared mathematical structure != shared physical meaning.
Formal similarity != causal equivalence != empirical truth.

32. FET v1.0

FET is the bounded emergency-reflex theory.

Canonical abstract emergency contribution:

    G_emg =
        HazardGate(validated_hazard_state, EmergencyAdmissionContract)
        o R_safe

Internal regulation state can raise monitoring priority but cannot self-authorize emergency power.

33. SCT v1.0

SCT is Garden's structural/coherence truth-seeking framework.

It uses calibrated self-failure assessment, OOD handling, testability/identifiability gates, signal fusion, guarded
experimentation, verifier diversity and calibrated abstention.

UNKNOWN / INCONCLUSIVE / NOT_APPLICABLE can be correct results.

SCT is not a universal truth oracle.

34. THEORY COMPOSITION

The seven theories share a typed composition architecture, not one mandatory universal equation.

Generic coupled-state view:

    E_T z =
        F_VSM(z,b,c)
        + G_RPM(z,u,c)
        + G_PPM(z,u,c)
        + G_FET(validated_hazard, admission, c)
        + Q
        + Xi

If TFT applies:

    E_T z + Div_D J_z =
        F_VSM + G_RPM + G_PPM + G_FET + Q + Xi

SRT supplies stratum/context structure.
SCT supplies structural/coherence discipline.
Coupling order is explicit when operators do not commute.

43. EXPLANATION AND CONTESTABILITY

44. LAW / POLICY / CONFLICT
