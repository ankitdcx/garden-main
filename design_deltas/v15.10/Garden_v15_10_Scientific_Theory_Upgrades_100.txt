Garden v15.10 — Scientific Theory Upgrade Register

100 candidate upgrades across 20 established theory families | Research candidate | 9 October 2026

Evidence boundary. These are 100 explicitly formulated research hypotheses, NOT 100 validated discoveries. No claim of worldwide novelty, improved empirical accuracy, or successful laboratory testing is made. Mathematical structures are proposals; each requires derivation, literature novelty review, baseline comparison and independent experiments.

A. Universal upgrade mechanism

1. Freeze source: Pin theory version, primary equations, observations, units, domain and counterexamples; preserve originals in the Catalogue.

2. Map the situation: Represent TIME, SPACE, THING, EVENT, ACTION, AGENCY, RULE, VALUE, CONTEXT, CLAIM. Mark AGENCY inapplicable for non-agent physical systems rather than inventing agency.

3. Type relationships: Use applicable members of the 24 relation registry; distinguish causal, correlational, dependency, provenance and contradiction claims. Never force all 24 onto every theory.

4. Instantiate seven forms: CONSTRUCT the system; CONTRACT assumptions; STATE its variables; RELATION the dependencies; PROCESS evolution; RULE invariants; PROJECTION coarse-grained observables.

5. Apply facets and algebras: Check scope, identity, evidence, dependencies, effects, resources, audit and recovery. Use typed GSL, compare/reason and finite process compositions; authority and human-boundary rules govern research actions, not physical laws.

6. Identify abstraction loss: Find hidden state, covariance, topology, delays or switching lost by the current projection. Require an explicit counterexample or residual.

7. Construct candidate: Add a minimal state/term with dimensions, sign/positivity and conservation constraints; derive limiting behavior recovering the baseline.

8. Attack and compare: Check identifiability, invariants, extreme limits, alternative mechanisms, numerical stability and withheld datasets; test against strong existing models, not only the textbook baseline.

9. Independent verification: Separate proof, simulation, empirical fit, novelty and practical utility statuses; UNKNOWN never means PASS.

10. Controlled admission: Record evidence receipts and falsifiers; admit only through the relevant scientific and Garden review pathways. Generated graph views cannot self-admit.

B. Reusable mathematical candidate forms

Baseline: y_dot = f(y,u;theta). Full state x=(y,z), with projection A(x)=y.

Memory: y_dot = f(y,u)+B z; z_dot = -(z-z_eq(y,u))/tau.

Heterogeneity: E[f(X)] = f(E[X]) + 1/2 Hessian(f):Cov(X) + O(third moments).

Topology: y_dot = f(y,u,q), where q is a measured connectivity/order statistic and q_dot=g(q,y,u).

Delay: y_dot = f(y,u) + integral_0^infty K(s) g(y(t-s),u(t-s)) ds.

Switching: y_dot = f_h(y,u); h follows measured transitions with hysteresis.

Test: Delta_loss = held-out error(baseline) - held-out error(candidate); positive Delta_loss only counts after matched complexity and budget controls.

E. EXPLICIT v15.10 ABSTRACTION MAPPING AND HONESTY AUDIT

This appendix clarifies how the 100 candidates use Garden abstractions. It does not claim that 100 independent derivations, experimental tests, or novelty reviews have been performed.

1. TEN CORE OBJECTS (roles in every candidate)
TIME: observation interval, lag, horizon, timescale.
SPACE: spatial domain, interfaces, compartments, network position; explicitly NOT_APPLICABLE when no spatial structure matters.
THING: physical species, device, organism, or mathematical system.
EVENT: reaction, transition, crack advance, failure, infection, or observation.
ACTION: intervention or applied input (heating, charging, dosing); NOT_APPLICABLE if no intervention.
AGENCY: decision-capable experimenter/controller when relevant; NOT_APPLICABLE for molecules or particles themselves.
RULE: conservation law, governing equation, physical boundary or admissibility condition.
VALUE: quantified objective such as accuracy, safety, yield, durability or cost; not a substitute for evidence.
CONTEXT: environment, regime, scale, initial conditions, applicability limits.
CLAIM: baseline prediction, proposed increment, falsifier, evidence status.

2. EXACT 24 RELATION REGISTRY
identifies, causes, governs, values, frames, acts, obeys, assesses, contextualizes, controls, partOf, dependsOn, owns, delegates, references, derivedFrom, equivalentTo, contradicts, supports, blocks, hasHypothesis, supersedes, conflictsWith, originatesFrom.
Only type-valid applicable relations are instantiated. For example, hidden state dependsOn prior exposure; a measurement supports a claim; a counterexample contradicts a prediction; an equation derivedFrom a set of assumptions. 'causes' requires causal support, not correlation. 'owns' and 'delegates' concern accountable source/authority, not a molecule's intentions.

3. SEVEN DESIGN FORMS
CONSTRUCT: bounded scientific system and candidate record.
CONTRACT: inputs, units, preconditions, observables, applicability and falsification.
STATE: measured baseline variables and proposed hidden variables.
RELATION: typed dependency and provenance edges.
PROCESS: state transition, intervention, simulation, experimental workflow.
RULE: equations, invariants, nonnegativity, conservation and admission constraints.
PROJECTION: fine-grained system -> reduced theory, including loss and error.
