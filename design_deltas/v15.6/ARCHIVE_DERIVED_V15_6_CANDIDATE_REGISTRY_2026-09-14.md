## D. Registration semantics

`REGISTERED_FOR_V15_6 != RATIFIED != CANONICAL != IMPLEMENTED != CERTIFIED`

The candidate pool intentionally preserves worthy mechanisms even when the likely final disposition is one of:

- direct v15.6 semantic delta;
- bounded profile over an existing owner;
- implementation hardening rather than source-design change;
- refinement/subcandidate of another v15.6 item;
- exact-definition recovery followed by equivalence review;
- NO_CHANGE because current Garden already covers the mechanism.

This prevents two opposite errors: losing a useful historical mechanism, or bloating Garden by promoting every historical name as a new module.

## E. Promotion boundary

Every item still requires the ordinary Garden successor gates appropriate to its class, including:

- exact-source recovery where definition is incomplete;
- whole-source equivalence/collision review;
- GSL-COMPARE including DO_NOTHING and stronger alternatives;
- minimal-owner selection and no duplicate semantics/topology;
- independent same-target review and cross-examination;
- rights/privacy/authority analysis;
- executable tests/proofs/empirical evidence where applicable;
- cross-document propagation and reference/catalogue closure;
- explicit human canonical promotion.

Until those gates pass, **Garden v15.5 remains the canonical predecessor and these items remain Garden v15.6 candidates only.**
