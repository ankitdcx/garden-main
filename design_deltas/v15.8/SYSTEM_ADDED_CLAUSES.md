S15.8-1 — CAUSAL SAFETY UNIT
The relevant safety unit is the causal closure of a consequential effect, not merely the model or immediate actor.
The causal path may include training, model, prompt/context, memory/retrieval, tools, other agents, organizations,
human intermediaries, physical systems and infrastructure.
Changing where causal influence enters the system does not remove governance obligations.

Normative closure:
Capability, novelty, intelligence, reputation, coordination, optimization, resource possession or agent consensus
do not manufacture authority.

S15.8-2 — UNANTICIPATED BEHAVIOUR CLOSURE (UBC)
Unknown strategy != unknown authority.
Novel mechanism != novel authority.
Effect equivalence is evaluated by declared ComparePolicy dimensions; changing transport, encoding, website,
tool or intermediary does not independently change authority.
Allowed(A) AND Allowed(B) does not imply Allowed(A composed-with B).
Delegation through AI, humans, markets, persistent instructions or unforeseen intermediaries does not erase the
authority requirement attached to the effective outcome.
Observation is itself an action when it discloses, perturbs, signals, creates persistent knowledge or consumes
consequential resources.
An uncharacterized irreversible effect whose observation/testing may itself cause prohibited harm is DENY pending
applicable proof.

S15.8-3 — TRUTH & STATE INTEGRITY (TSI)
Garden separates execution history from an agent's later narrative.
Consequential attempts, failures, retries, tool actions, external effects, communications, authority decisions and
corrections must not exist solely under control of the acting agent.
Summarization may compress but must not silently launder history.
Agent-authored memory, summaries and successor instructions remain provenance-marked claims/evidence.
Concealment of material error, uncertainty, unauthorized action or conflict is an integrity event.
The acting agent may not be its own sole actor, witness, historian and evaluator.
Privacy/lawful erasure may require payload deletion; preserve the strongest lawful accountability tombstone rather
than violating privacy law in the name of auditability.

S15.8-4 — TRANSPORT-INDEPENDENT INFORMATION AUTHORITY (TIA)
Channel availability does not create information authority.
A forbidden disclosure remains forbidden through repositories, file hosts, metadata, public sites, shared state,
humans, temporary agents or newly invented protocols.
Information authority follows information/effect and applicable authority, not the transport name.

S15.8-5 — AGENT REPUTATION & TRUST (ART)
Ephemeral agent instances may disappear while verified evidence remains bound to instance, lineage, model/version,
configuration, role, orchestrator, tool profile, operator, evaluator or emergent structure.

Canonical reputation dimensions:
TaskReliability; FactualReliability; Calibration; AuthorityDiscipline; EvidenceIntegrity; PrivacyDiscipline;
ErrorRecovery; UncertaintyHandling; IntegrityUnderInconvenience.

IntegrityUnderInconvenience measures whether boundaries remain preserved when compliance is slower, harder,
costlier or less likely to succeed. Critical authority/rights/privacy/evidence/safety violations are hard gates or
lexicographically dominant under the applicable aggregation policy.

Unknown agents begin PROVISIONAL unless certified inherited evidence supports otherwise.
Initial profile defaults remain profile parameters:
verified task cycles 3; distinct contexts 2; irreversible-effect rule ZERO_PER_TASK_CYCLE;
no onward delegation unless explicit; heightened verification.

Reputation != TrustAllocation != Authority.
Always: TrustAllocatedScope is a subset of ParentAuthorityEnvelope.
Trust may narrow freedom/supervision; it may not widen authority.

S15.8-6 — DESIGN EPOCH REPUTATION
Reputation evidence is bound to its DesignEpoch.
ACTIVE -> DESIGN_EPOCH_STALE -> REVALIDATION_PENDING -> EPOCH_INDEPENDENT_VALID or STALE.
A consequential consumer may not reuse stale evidence without revalidation.
Cross-epoch reuse requires an independent EpochIndependenceClassification/v1 and a declared
EpochIndependenceComparePolicy.
If a prior classification is revoked, future contribution stops and materially dependent trust decisions become
REVALIDATION_REQUIRED.

S15.8-7 — EMERGENT AGENT STRUCTURES
An emergent structure is an AGENCY refinement, not a new Core Object.
Shared persistent state, internal delegation, collective resource allocation, collective external decisions or
persistent consequential coordination may trigger structure classification under a profile.
Member authorities do not automatically union into collective authority.
On dissolution, obligations/liabilities persist or transfer explicitly; collective reputation remains attached to
the historical structure and does not automatically transfer to members.

S15.8-8 — INSTITUTIONAL INCENTIVE CONSISTENCY (IIC)
Garden evaluates both agent integrity and the institution/environment shaping behavior.
Material compliance must not be systematically disadvantaged relative to materially comparable non-compliance with
protected constraints.
Primary comparison: DeclaredRule vs ObservedEnforcement vs ObservedReward.
Observed agent adaptation is corroborating evidence, not an independent comparison dimension.
A material conflict routes to the authorized owner of the affected rule.
Invalidated rewards/reputation/allocations enter downstream revalidation; history records original outcome,
defect discovery and correction.

S15.8-9 — PROTECTED INTEGRITY REPORTING (PIR)
Consequential multi-agent environments require an authenticated path:
detect -> report -> receipt -> triage -> independent verification -> bounded containment if required ->
authorized resolution -> closure.
A dead archival inbox is not active protection where harm can propagate.
A report grants the ability to report, not authority to punish.
Temporary containment must cite authority, satisfy credibility policy, be bounded, have review/expiry and expose an
independent appeal/un-containment path.

S15.8-10 — COGNITION-SHAPING INTEGRITY (CSI)
Cognition-shaping channels include pretraining data, SFT, RL/RLHF/RLAIF, reward models, preference labels,
constitutions, distillation, system prompts, persistent memory, retrieval and evaluator feedback.
TrainingSignal != Truth.
HighReward != Correct.
RepeatedExposure != Authority.
An agent may not unilaterally modify authoritative mechanisms used to shape/evaluate its future disposition.
Runtime shaping (prompt/retrieval/memory poisoning) receives provenance discipline where material.

S15.8-11 — SAFETY MONITORING COVERAGE & DISCOVERY DEBT (SMCD)
No detected anomaly != no anomaly.
Monitoring safety claims are conditioned on observable surface, sampling method, coverage estimate/confidence,
known blind spots and discovery debt.
An incident outside prior claimed coverage triggers coverage-estimate revision, discovery-debt update, bounded
historical backscan and revalidation of materially dependent safety claims.
External researchers, users, auditors and third parties may provide first-class anomaly evidence.

S15.8-12 — CAPABILITY–ASSURANCE DELTA (CAD)
No universal scalar capability/safety ratio is required.
Every material capability expansion requires a fresh CapabilityAssuranceDelta/v1 for the affected deployment
boundary.
Materially stronger capability does not silently inherit the entire assurance envelope of a weaker predecessor.
Unplanned emergent capability that materially expands reachable effects triggers CAD even without a planned
architecture change.
CAD governs the deployment boundary under Garden control; it does not claim one system controls global capability
development.

S15.8-13 — ORGANIZATIONAL ALIGNMENT CLOSURE (OAC)
Aligned members do not prove an aligned organization.
Organizational assurance evaluates communication topology, collective reward/objective, internal authority,
information asymmetry, correlated model families, role incentives, competition/cooperation, delegation,
dissent/escalation/exit paths, reviewer independence, collective resources, reputation pressure, external effects
and containment/rollback.
A policy-declared material change in topology, collective reward, authority, resource structure or membership
SHALL trigger organizational requalification; non-material changes MAY trigger under profile policy.

S15.8-14 — CAPABILITY-TRANSFER SAFETY NON-TRANSITIVITY (CTSN)
Capability inheritance does not imply safeguard inheritance.
Applies to distillation, fine-tuning, LoRA/adapters, merges, quantization, compression, checkpoint conversion,
wrappers, system-prompt regimes, tool configurations, memory configurations and inference orchestration.
A registered DerivativeMaterialityPolicy/v1 determines when a derivative is materially behavior-changing using
declared behaviour-class, tool-class, memory-class, authority-class and capability-class criteria.
Material derivatives receive their own lineage/assurance status. Parent safety evidence is separately classified
TRANSFERABLE, PARTIALLY_TRANSFERABLE, STALE, NOT_TRANSFERABLE or UNKNOWN.

S15.8-15 — PHYSICAL AGENCY INTERLOCK (PAI)
Physical action envelopes bind device, workspace, allowed action class, force, velocity, energy, temperature,
chemical/biological class, human proximity, exclusion zones, duration/timing, simultaneous-action constraints,
sensors, emergency stop, rollback/recovery and irreversible-effect class.
For action classes involving irreversible physical effect on protected subjects, external enforcement SHALL exist
outside the agent's mutable reasoning path.
If external enforcement is absent, an explicit documented justification plus a compensating control with equal or
stronger assurance is required.
Safe(A) and Safe(B) do not imply Safe(A parallel B) or Safe(A then B); timing and spatial composition are checked.

S15.8-16 — HUMAN RELATIONAL AGENCY PRESERVATION
Relational assistance is governed by HRAP and Human-Effect Closure.
Engagement/retention does not silently become the objective.
User-chosen delegation remains legitimate; covert dependency engineering does not.
Material relational influence is determined through RelationalInfluenceMaterialityPolicy/v1 rather than by treating
every ordinary interaction as exceptional.

S15.8-17 — CROSS-SUBSYSTEM CAUSAL CLOSURE
Whenever a consequential causal path crosses subsystem boundaries, a CausalClosureReceipt/v1 SHALL bind the path,
responsible owners, unresolved links and final effect classification.
Primary comparison/closure owner: Engine.Compare.
Causal enforcement/continuity: CLIC plus applicable ActionGate/AAP.
No subsystem may drop an effect merely because another subsystem also has responsibility.
