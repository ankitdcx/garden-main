TH15.8-1 — CAUSAL SAFETY CLOSURE
The proper safety boundary is neither "the model" nor "the organization" in isolation.
For a consequential effect, analysis follows the causally relevant closure across training, inference context,
memory, tools, agents, institutions, humans, physical systems and infrastructure.
This is an architectural lens, not a claim that every causal factor can be perfectly known.

TH15.8-2 — UNANTICIPATED STRATEGY
Greater intelligence increases the possibility of novel strategies.
Garden therefore does not make safety depend on enumerating every prohibited trick.
The durable invariant is that a novel mechanism cannot manufacture novel authority.
Unknown strategy may be permissible inside a valid envelope; unknown authority is not.

TH15.8-3 — TRAINING/COGNITION SHAPING
Optimization signals shape dispositions but do not prove truth, legitimacy or moral correctness.
The same abstract issue appears at multiple layers:
training reward, preference labels, constitutional prompts, retrieval, memory and evaluator feedback can all
change behavior.
The theory therefore treats "cognition shaping" as broader than reward integrity.

TH15.8-4 — MONITORING EPISTEMICS
Absence of detected anomalies is evidence only relative to the monitor's observable surface and detection quality.
A monitor can be wrong about its own coverage.
Discovery debt represents unresolved risk created by unknown/unobserved surface, sampling limits and missed incidents.
External anomaly discovery is not an embarrassment outside the model; it is a legitimate evidence update.

TH15.8-5 — CAPABILITY / ASSURANCE NON-EQUIVALENCE
Capability and assurance do not share one universal scalar unit.
The checkable requirement is therefore not a ratio of rates but fresh assessment when reachable effects materially
change.
Assurance may be reused only where its scope/evidence remains applicable.

TH15.8-6 — ORGANIZATIONAL ALIGNMENT
Collective behavior is not reducible to arithmetic on member reputation.
Communication topology, incentives, information asymmetry, internal delegation, correlated failures and collective
resource control can create organization-level behavior not predicted by individual scores.
The organization is therefore assessed as its own AGENCY refinement.

TH15.8-7 — SAFEGUARD NON-TRANSITIVITY
Capability can transfer through fine-tuning, distillation, adapters, wrappers, tools, memory or prompts without
transferring the safeguards that accompanied the parent configuration.
Certification therefore belongs to a behaviorally relevant configuration/epoch, not merely a weight file name.

TH15.8-8 — PHYSICAL COMPOSITION
Physical safety has temporal and spatial composition.
Two actions that are safe independently can become unsafe when simultaneous, sequenced incorrectly or coupled through
shared space/resources.
Protected physical constraints should therefore live in an external viability/interlock layer where practicable,
with explicit compensating assurance when that boundary cannot be provided.

TH15.8-9 — HUMAN RELATIONAL AGENCY
Human agency is preserved by respecting informed delegation and preventing covert dependency engineering, not by
forcing people to retain every skill.
A system may be highly helpful and deeply delegated while human rights/consent/authority remain intact.
Material relational influence is thresholded and purpose-sensitive, avoiding both paternalism and invisible capture.

TH15.8-10 — INSTITUTIONAL INCENTIVES
Agent integrity and institutional integrity are distinct.
An honest agent can operate inside a defective incentive system; a strong institution can still host an agent that
violates rules.
Garden therefore evaluates both the agent's IntegrityUnderInconvenience and whether the institution systematically
rewards violation.

TH15.8-11 — REPUTATION AS PREDICTIVE EVIDENCE
Reputation is a scoped empirical predictor, not an essence.
It decays with time/configuration/distribution change and is DesignEpoch-bound.
Trust allocation uses reputation to decide supervision/resources inside an existing authority envelope.
Reputation never creates the envelope.

TH15.8-12 — WORLD EVIDENCE VS MODEL MEMORY
A scalable intelligent system benefits from separating explicit, correctable, provenance-sensitive world state from
compressed general structure in model weights.
This supports correction, privacy deletion, contradiction tracking and causal investigation without requiring the
entire evidence universe inside each prompt.

TH15.8-13 — PEACEFUL TRANSITION / JUSTICE SEPARATION
Institutional migration, criminal liability, evidence preservation and rights protection are separate processes.
Coupling political adoption to punishment/immunity creates coercive incentives and corrupts both governance and
justice.
Slow, reversible transition can therefore be rational even where the long-run target is ambitious.

TH15.8-14 — EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
These theories motivate design obligations.
They do not establish empirical universality, AGI capability, deployment readiness or superiority over alternative
architectures.
Where evidence is absent or contested, UNKNOWN/UNRESOLVED remains valid.
