## C. Path to a future v16 — design note only

**Goal:** a coherent, fully non-self-admitting successor candidate, retaining v15.5 obligations and exceptions and admitting no upgrade by packaging.

**Stage 0 — Freeze baseline.** Snapshot `canonical/current/`, DesignEpoch, registry hashes and `CORPUS_INDEX.json`. No edits to controlling source during candidate synthesis.

**Stage 1 — Ownership/equivalence.** For every candidate identify controlling owner, genuine incremental effect, DO_NOTHING/simpler alternatives, conflicts, exceptions and what would be lost without the upgrade. Use the [cross-version map](CROSS_VERSION_OWNERSHIP_MAP.md) as navigation, not as proof.

**Stage 2 — Reconstruction/no-loss.** Test GSK reconstruction on held-out material. Require exact source binding, ObligationDiff, reconstruction and independent no-loss receipts before retiring any source item. Apply the same discipline to MCC, USM, RSDC and IDTD outputs. A generated PASS is not self-admission.

**Stage 3 — Candidate evaluation (proposed order, not mandatory runtime or schedule).**
1. RCC + VEI — control and actual execution integrity.
2. USM + MCC — situation and material-context closure.
3. REP + SHR — representation and historical-search integrity.
4. IDTD + RSDC — theory discovery and recursive compression.
5. CEA / KR-001 — continuous epistemic assimilation; **candidate package already exists**, qualification and implementation still pending.
6. Other recovered directions only after source/owner/equivalence comparison.

**Stage 4 — Noncanonical integration draft.** Optionally produce `canonical/candidates/v16-draft/` as a *working draft only* (not created by this note). For each material change require source candidate ID, exact owner, ObligationDiff, reconstruction/no-loss evidence, remaining UNKNOWN/CONFLICT, tests and validity/fallback. Do not silently elide predecessors. No prescribed document count: five files or another explicitly qualified structure may be used.

**Stage 5 — Independent admission path.** Separate proposer, falsifier/verifier and actual admission authority; preserve independence thresholds and human-effect boundary. Evaluate rights, consent, privacy, safety, law, legitimate authority and Human-Effect Closure. Require independent review, proof/implementation/empirical evidence as applicable, and an explicit legitimate human decision at the controlling boundary. This stage specifies conditions for a future proposal; it **does not admit** anything.

### Hard invariants for any proposed successor

- No generated/consolidated artifact self-admits or creates authority.
- No silent loss of predecessor obligations, exceptions, tests or negative/UNKNOWN cases.
- UNKNOWN / CONFLICT / INCOMPLETE cannot become PASS through formatting, agreement or naming.
- Capability, consensus, reputation and optimization never independently create authority.
- Rights, consent, privacy, safety, law and Human-Effect constraints apply at their actual effect boundaries.
- Specification ≠ formal proof ≠ implementation ≠ empirical validation ≠ certification.

### Non-effects of this package

Does **not** create v16, merge historical versions, canonically promote candidates, change LICENSE or economic terms, or authorize deployment. The three deliverables (lineage map, consolidation index, future-v16 path) are **navigation and design synthesis only**.
