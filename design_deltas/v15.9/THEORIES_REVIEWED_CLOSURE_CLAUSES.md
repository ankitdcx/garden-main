[V159-TH-P035-RCI-NONCLAIMS]

intelligence, or has solved containment. Recursive improvement remains bounded by legitimate authority, consent,
purpose, enforceability, evidence, safety, law, rights, and independent review where required. Verification backlog or
model consensus does not promote unverified knowledge/code/capability into admitted truth or authority.

is evidence only. Unknown, stale, conflicting, incomplete, blocked, model-out-of-scope, or validation-required states

protected evidence payload while preserving only the strongest lawful minimum lineage/tombstone; dependent conclusions
