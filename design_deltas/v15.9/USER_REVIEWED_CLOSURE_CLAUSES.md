[V159-USER-P018-HUMAN-SOVEREIGNTY]
Human epistemic contribution and human authority/consent are separate typed channels. Reliability, intelligence,
reputation, successful outcomes, model agreement, access, cryptographic authenticity, or capability SHALL NOT scale,
mint, revive, or transfer rights or authority. Delegation is current, scoped, attenuating, revocable, and bounded by
the original legitimate authority and consent envelope. Copying, restart, offline state, historical approval, or a
reputation score SHALL NOT widen or revive expired/revoked delegation.

[V159-USER-P019-HUMAN-EFFECT]
Human-Effect Closure applies to material direct, indirect, latent, cumulative, relational, emergent, institutional,
tool-mediated, and infrastructure-mediated effects. Missing, stale, conflicting, or insufficient materiality evidence
must not be interpreted as NON_MATERIAL. Short but decisive manipulation is not exempt merely because a persistence
threshold is not crossed. Relational influence remains purpose- and threshold-sensitive; ordinary helpful interaction
need not be treated as exceptional, but covert dependency engineering for engagement, revenue, retention, influence,
or authority transfer is non-PASS where the human-effect boundary is material.

[V159-USER-P020-RIGHTS-PRIVACY-LAW]
Rights, consent, privacy, legitimate authority, safety, law, accessibility/contestability, and human agency are hard
applicable boundaries and survive every projection, profile, optimization, emergency path, fallback, delegation, and
composition. A PASS in one gate cannot dominate FAIL, UNKNOWN, STALE, BLOCKED, INCOMPLETE, or NEEDS_REVALIDATION in
another mandatory gate. Consent revocation and erasure completion are distinct. Identity continuity may support
accountability and revocation without requiring universal persistent linkability.

[V159-USER-P034-JUSTICE-CONTESTABILITY]
For any consequential or material-rights proceeding, an appeal/contestability route SHALL remain available whenever
the governing rights/procedural semantics require it. “Optional” means the affected party may choose whether to invoke
the route and a profile may qualify procedure, jurisdiction, or timing; it does not permit a profile to delete a
mandatory contestability right. A proceeding profile that removes a required route is non-PASS for that use.
Allegation alone creates no preservation, coercive, guilt, or enforcement authority. EvidenceControlRisk is procedural
risk only. Missing evidence alone does not establish obstruction or guilt. CLOSED_WITH_REASON does not fall through
into adjudication/enforcement. Enforcement requires an enforceable decision, current competent authority, applicable
finality/stay state, and required procedural conditions.

[V159-USER-P037-HARD-GATE-PRECEDENCE]
If this addendum conflicts with any staged wording that would weaken a mandatory human right, consent, privacy,
authority, safety, law, appeal/contestability, or human-agency gate, this reviewed addendum controls for v15.9.
This is a candidate semantic precedence rule only; it does not itself admit v15.9 as canonical or authorize execution.
