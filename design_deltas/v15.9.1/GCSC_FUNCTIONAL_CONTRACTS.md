- UNKNOWN, missing semantic bindings, ambiguous joins, incomplete coverage, and non-executable outputs remain non-PASS; report resource-bound INCOMPLETE rather than silently certifying coverage.

- GCSC is a bounded meta-design, **not** an eighth core theory. It does not supersede specialist theory equations, domain science, human/rights/safety/authority contracts, or evidence qualification.
- Generated tests/proof obligations are obligations or artifacts, not proof of their own adequacy or correctness.
- Source-derived projections can supersede only specifically designated generated/projection mechanisms through exact scoped clauses, never broad predecessor elision.
