[TH-GCSC-1591] GCSC AS META-DESIGN / COVERAGE THEORY
GCSC is not an eighth Garden core theory and does not change SRT/VSM/RPM/PPM/TFT/FET/SCT. It is a bounded generative
meta-design for exploring the semantic possibility space implied by GSL and Garden contracts. SAL constrains meaningful
construction; materiality/composition/property oracles detect consequential interactions; SAC derives candidate
assurance artifacts. Domain/theory truth still requires its ordinary evidence, proof and applicability routes.

The current primitive set is not expanded merely because a reviewer proposes NOT/absence, temporal ordering, quantity,
modality, quantification, intent, collective agency, speech acts, possible worlds, STATE or rule-priority relations.
Each remains a primitive candidate until existing objects/relations/qualifiers are shown unable to represent the
distinction without semantic loss or pathological complexity.
