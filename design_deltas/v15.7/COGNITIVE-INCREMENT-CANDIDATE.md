## 1. Active explanation competition
Activate when unresolved explanations would lead to different decisions, not on every question.
- Maintain competing hypotheses with assumptions, predictions, provenance and contradictions.
- Preserve the possibility that all current hypotheses are inadequate; this is not a fabricated alternative with an invented probability.
- Choose the next question, measurement, simulation or reasoning step through a bound decision method. Declare uncertainty model, information-value estimate or heuristic, cost/risk treatment, stopping conditions and budget.
- Declare distinguishing observations before collecting results.
- Update hypotheses from observations, retaining uncertainty when results do not distinguish them.
- Apply existing authority, privacy and budget requirements to every experiment.

Reconciliation input: identify the exact existing host (AAP, Reason or another decision contract) for selection AND termination. If none suffices, record EXTENSION_REQUIRED or NEW_CONTRACT_REQUIRED with justification; do not assume a host exists.
Acceptance: on held-out cases with misleading initial explanations, improve diagnosis or decision quality against the existing approach under matched budgets. Include ambiguous evidence, missing hypotheses and unnecessary-investigation cases.
Regression: no detected unacceptable increase in needless investigation, incorrect certainty, cost or latency under predeclared criteria and declared coverage.

## 2. Qualified skill acquisition
After a completed task:
- Extract a candidate parameterized routine with prerequisites, expected effects, dependencies and failure conditions.
- Preserve source execution, provenance and evaluation evidence.
- Separate successful execution from demonstrated generality.
- Test changed inputs, boundary conditions and relevant counterexamples before wider reuse.
- Record qualification scope; abstain or seek further qualification outside it.
- Preserve failed approaches with their applicable conditions; reconsider when relevant conditions change.
- Invalidate affected qualifications when tools, assumptions or dependencies change.
- Learning a skill never expands authority or resets execution budgets.

Reconciliation input: map the parameterized routine to existing Procedure, Plan or Capability representations, or justify an extension/new contract. Candidate names are not declarations that those exact source types exist.
Required bounded qualification scope: supported inputs, operating conditions, dependencies, exclusions and evidence coverage. Bind concrete acceptance/reliability criteria and uncertainty treatment for the tested scope. Resolve representation before affected admission; do not defer this essential scope behind the broader deferred topic.
Acceptance: useful reuse on unseen tasks against solving afresh, including extraction/qualification costs.
Regression: test overgeneralization, stale prerequisites, misleading success records and harmful reuse; report errors and resource costs separately.

## 5. Evaluation ownership, gain and regression
An authorized evaluation owner protected from proposer modification defines and versions held-out distributions, criteria and evaluation configuration. Preserve separation from practice and improvement selection; record distribution coverage limits and trust dependencies.
Evaluate explanation competition and skill acquisition separately AND together. Combined gains cannot hide a harmful component.
Each mechanism/amendment needs:
- Intended benefit on held-out cases, or evidence supporting NO_CHANGE.
- No detected unacceptable regression on affected task families and selected neighboring tasks within declared coverage/tolerances.
- Predeclared correctness, safety and resource criteria; aggregate gains cannot conceal mandatory-invariant failure.
- Attribution by enabling/disabling the mechanism where feasible.
Include qualification costs, failures and negative results. Testing does not prove universal absence of regression.

Valid outcomes include existing coverage, smaller amendment, partial admission, rejection and blocked scope.
