## Integrated loop and failure
Observe → represent → predict → plan → authorize → act → evaluate → update.
Preserve provenance, uncertainty, dependencies, permissions and budgets across transitions. Learning cannot change its permissions or acceptance criteria. Updates remain provisional until qualified and must be rejectable/reversible under existing recovery rules.
Test components separately and together. If components pass but the loop fails, eligible component admission may proceed only with independently satisfied dependencies; integrated admission is BLOCKED and a composition finding is recorded. Test pass alone is not activation.
