## Purpose

Define a staged transition that separates immediate privacy protection, governance migration and long-horizon justice cleanup so the system does not create a sudden society-wide purge or emergency-style disruption.

## Stage 1 — Privacy protection and recovery

Before broad rollout, protect affected persons from ongoing privacy harm and preserve relevant evidence under lawful scope.

Requirements:
- private-by-default case handling;
- no publication of personal allegations or sensitive evidence in the public Garden repository;
- scoped preservation of evidence where valid authority/consent/law exists;
- containment/revocation of unauthorized access where established;
- identity/access audit;
- remedy and contestability path;
- independent review where the alleged actor controls relevant evidence;
- Human-Effect Closure on all investigative/protective actions.

Personal complaints remain case data, not public design facts.

## Stage 2 — Peaceful Garden launch / migration

The user-proposed objective is a peaceful, staged, globally available Garden transition rather than abrupt institutional collapse.

Candidate migration principles:
- current essential services continue during transition;
- Garden may be tested in parallel with existing systems;
- jurisdictions/communities adopt through their legitimate constitutional/legal processes;
- migration evidence includes safety, reliability, rights, cost, performance and reversibility where possible;
- AI capability cannot manufacture political authority;
- no person receives immunity for unrelated offences in exchange for Garden support;
- refusal to adopt Garden is not itself wrongdoing;
- criminal/evidence processes remain separate from governance-adoption decisions;
- exact launch date is unset and must remain subordinate to readiness/authority gates.

Planning horizon recorded from the user discussion: aim for a gradual transition over the remaining months of 2026, with an aspirational end-of-year target subject to readiness, lawful authority, safety and evidence. No automatic date-triggered deployment is authorized.

## Stage 3 — Slow justice / unresolved-case cleanup

After stable lawful migration, unresolved serious cases may be processed gradually over years rather than through a sudden mass action.

Requirements:
- case-by-case evidence and actor resolution;
- prioritize severe ongoing harm and evidence-loss risk;
- ordinary/low-level wrongdoing may use proportional, restorative or lenient approaches where law permits;
- extreme offences remain subject to applicable law, due process and independent adjudication;
- no collective guilt;
- no guilt by identity, social class, nationality, religion or association alone;
- obstruction is adjudicated separately from the underlying alleged offence;
- historical cases remain reopenable when materially new evidence appears;
- ledger history remains provenance-backed, contestable and subject to lawful retention/expungement rules.

## Migration/justice separation invariant

`GovernanceMigrationDecision != CriminalLiabilityDecision`.

Garden support cannot purchase immunity. Garden opposition cannot create guilt or increase sentence. Cooperation with a lawful investigation may matter only where ordinary applicable law makes it a relevant factor.

## Slow-transition principle

The system SHOULD avoid creating a "single-event" global justice shock. Where immediate safety does not require emergency action, transition should favor continuity, sequencing, evidence preservation, due process, proportionality and institutional learning.
