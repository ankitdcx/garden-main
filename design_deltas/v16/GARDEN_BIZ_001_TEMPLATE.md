## How to instantiate a template

Each of the 38 non-detailed rows in the [industry register](GARDEN_BIZ_001_INDUSTRY_REGISTER.md) is a seeded industry template: it already specifies the industry, common platform, selectable modules, proposed differentiating process and critical validation. Combine that row with the reusable fields below. Do not create 38 duplicate files or repeat the shared process. Keep unknowns explicitly UNKNOWN, distinguish owner decisions from assistant proposals, and do not promote any row to detailed design without user-directed design work and express confirmation of the resulting design scope. Drafts and proposals may be prepared as requested. Use stable IDs; keep the register row linked to its own detailed document when one exists.

## Standard template (fill from user decisions)

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable business ID, industry row, working name, source date, owner decisions, revision, rights notice |
| Customer need | Target customers, use cases, value, alternatives, quality and price positioning |
| Product architecture | Common platform, module types, quantities, compatibility, fixed elements, constraints |
| Product catalog | Preset products, allowable custom configurations, variants, explicit exclusions, samples |
| Ingredient/material registry | Supplier, material, interface, unit, cost, yield, lead time, storage, shelf life, lot, safety and rights |
| Product specification | Drawings, recipes, formulas, pattern or BOM, process steps, acceptance tests; no invented formulas |
| Pricing | Price increments, target margin, taxes, total variable cost, customization/minimum batch, refund/return rules |
| Operating model | Production/assembly steps, order-to-delivery flow, capacity, quality gates, licensed partner vs in-house |
| Outlet/factory design | Space, equipment, setup cost, working capital, inventory, utility and safety needs |
| Staffing | Roles, training, wages, paid preparation/cleanup, shift and capacity constraints |
| Marketing and brand | Original identity, samples/display, monthly/seasonal collections if useful; rights clearance |
| Business economics | Assumption ledger, per-product margin, full profit and cash-flow model, sensitivity, break-even |
| Replication / franchise | Proven own operations, independent replication, local investment and responsibilities, brand/product/process license, training, quality and supplier controls; explicit fee or profit-share denominator, eligible expenses, loss treatment, reporting/audit, support, termination, written authorization and qualified pilot; terms UNKNOWN until designed |
| Verification | Industry-specific hard gates, measurable quality/safety, comparator, pilot, falsifiers, unresolved questions |
| Rights / publication | Protected original assets, authorship and sources, third-party rights, applicable patent/trademark/design/copyright options, confidential information and prior-disclosure record; no grant inferred |
| Governance | GSL conceptual mapping, Reason/Compare/Proof, Materiality/Human-Effect, Production/ActionGate, assurance, authority and revision history |

## Lifecycle

TEMPLATE → USER-DIRECTED DESIGN IN PROGRESS → DETAILED DESIGN → PROTOTYPE / QUALIFICATION → INDEPENDENT COMMERCIAL VALIDATION (only when actually supported).

NEXT DESIGN is a scheduling designation, not a maturity level; it remains TEMPLATE until design work actually occurs. Each advancement records evidence and explicit scope; no automatic launch, patent filing, rights transfer, commercial authorization, or canonical admission. Templates and design documents are proprietary only to the extent rights are legally owned or controlled. Garden sale and authorization conditions apply, subject to the proposed [YAHAPAR exclusion](YAHAPAR_BRAND_AND_RIGHTS.md) on this candidate branch. Future confidential formulas, manuals, drawings or potentially patentable technical details should be reviewed before public disclosure; public material cannot be made secret retroactively.
