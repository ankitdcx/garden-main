## 1. What changes in this candidate

The first concrete new candidate is **`GARDEN-HOME-001`**, an applied architecture/housing-system concept. It models the whole home as standardized 25-sq-ft 5 × 5 ft cells whose eligible shared edges accept an easily replaceable open/partition/thick non-load-bearing wall/optional-door interface. Walls may be added, removed or exchanged after construction with controlled safety checks; adjacency connections can be reassigned to produce changing rooms, corridors and functions; a **variable-count, variable-width, cost-optimized engineered structural support overlay** and configurable central circulation zone coexist with the user-selected interior. The earlier five-column arrangement is preserved only as a historical comparator.

This work makes Garden's **human-experience / physical-infrastructure design** scope more concrete. It is an *application/profile* of existing Garden concepts, not a mandate to deploy a building system and not a generalized claim to ownership of modular construction.
