## 1. Purpose

This register separates four questions that must never be collapsed:

1. When does the available evidence first show a mechanism or design family?
2. When and how was the relevant technical subject matter publicly disclosed?
3. Is a feature a plausible patent candidate after eligibility, novelty, inventive-step/non-obviousness, enablement and prior-art review?
4. Has an application actually been filed or a patent actually been granted?

A project label is not a patent claim. Patent analysis must be feature-by-feature and jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction.

## 5. Candidate-family triage

This is engineering triage, not a legal conclusion.

### A. Highest priority for professional patentability / claim-chart review

**GAIA Titanium / Diamond technical safety architecture**
- Technical character: hard-real-time actuator safety, safety-island separation, deterministic local veto, barrier/reachability checks, bounded authorization artifacts, hardware/RT execution.
- Main risk: substantial portions of the older Diamond framing were publicly disclosed early; BFT, CBF/PB-CBF, hard-real-time scheduling and cryptographic audit each have extensive external prior art.
- Review question: whether later combinations, local-vs-distributed authority separation, bounded token contracts, hardware safety-island execution or other later technical features add a novel and non-obvious technical contribution not already publicly disclosed.
- Status: mixed. Old disclosed features = `PUBLICLY_DISCLOSED / PRIOR_ART_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; genuinely later unpublished improvements, if any, = `PRIVATE_CANDIDATE / DO_NOT_PUBLICLY_DISCLOSE` until mapped.

**TRAIN technical safety substrate**
- Technical character: hardware/compiler/runtime safety mechanisms, asynchronous safety paths, controller/observer integration, safety ISA/compiler concepts and bounded actuation.
- Main risk: many component techniques have mature prior art; project-level novelty cannot be assumed from integration alone.
- Review question: identify exact combinations that produce a demonstrable technical effect beyond ordinary aggregation of known controls.
- Status: `FILING_DECISION_PENDING` at family level; feature-level disclosure mapping required.

**FET emergency fast-path technical implementation**
- Technical character: bounded state monitoring, thresholded emergency/asymmetry drive, pre-authorized hardcoded/precompiled emergency path and recovery condition.
- Main risk: emergency override, safety interlock and real-time fallback prior art is extensive.
- Review question: whether the exact technical admission/recovery architecture is novel and non-obvious in a concrete machine/system implementation.
- Status: `PUBLICLY_DISCLOSED / PRIOR_ART_REVIEW_REQUIRED` for already-published forms; later unpublished refinements remain private candidates.

**SCT technical claim families**
- Technical character varies by family; parser/graph/calibration, hybrid verification, infrastructure gating and signal-fusion implementations may have more technical character than abstract value/governance concepts.
- Main risk: the explicit proposed portfolio is already present in retained/public Garden material and many subtechniques have prior art.
- Status: `PUBLICLY_DISCLOSED / PRIOR_ART_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; no family may be represented as filed or granted without actual records.

### B. Potential candidates only when tied to a concrete technical effect

**GSL compiler/runtime and typed conformance mechanisms**
- Potentially stronger where claims are directed to concrete computer operation, verified compilation, security/authority enforcement, machine-executable conformance or measurable technical improvement.
- Weak where reduced to an abstract ontology, formal language, mathematical rule or mental/business method.
- Status: `FILING_DECISION_PENDING`; do not publish new enabling implementation detail before review.

**APAO / ReliabilityOS / multi-model routing / reviewer machinery**
- Potentially stronger for concrete distributed-systems reliability, resource control, failure recovery, security isolation or measurable compute/network effect.
- Weak where reduced to “use several AIs, compare them, select the best,” governance workflow or business/process logic.
- Status: `FILING_DECISION_PENDING` only for concrete technical implementations; broad concept classified `NON_PATENT_IP_OR_PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE` unless counsel identifies qualifying technical subject matter.

### C. Do not rely on patents as the primary moat

Broad Garden governance, political structure, economic/tax rules, justice philosophy, social-choice rules, general ethical principles, abstract theories of consciousness/physics, general “AI should verify evidence” principles, and other non-technical normative ideas should not be assumed patentable merely because they are original or valuable.

Protection, where available, may instead depend on copyright in expression/code, confidential know-how before disclosure, trademarks/identity, contract/licence, database rights where applicable, or concrete patentable technical implementations.

## 6. Public-disclosure risk map

1. **Europe / EPC:** treat public disclosure before filing as potentially novelty-destroying except narrow statutory exceptions. Do not assume an inventor grace period.
2. **United States:** an inventor-originated disclosure may fall within the one-year AIA grace-period framework, but exact subject matter and dates matter. Do not infer a deadline from a project name; map each claim to each disclosure.
3. **India:** sections 31–32 contain specific anticipation exceptions (certain notified exhibitions/learned-society papers and necessary public trial). They are not treated here as a general social-media grace period.
4. **PCT / foreign filing from India:** a resident in India must check section 39 and related rules before filing abroad/PCT. A first Indian filing followed by foreign/PCT priority normally interacts with the Paris/PCT 12-month priority window, but the exact filing strategy requires qualified counsel.

## 9. Current overall assessment

Garden contains multiple technically serious invention-candidate areas, but “all Garden ideas are patent-worthy” is not supportable from the current evidence. The strongest moat should be layered:

- patent review for concrete technical mechanisms;
- copyright for code/specification/expression;
- confidentiality/trade-secret discipline for unpublished know-how;
- the Garden conditional-use licence for permitted use;
- provenance and timestamp evidence;
- and explicit prevention of accidental pre-filing disclosure.

The most urgent risk is not lack of candidate material. It is that early public disclosure may already narrow rights for some older features while future improvements could still be unnecessarily disclosed before a filing decision. The repository process must therefore optimize for feature-level evidence and confidentiality, not maximal public claim publication.
