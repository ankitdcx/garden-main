# GAIA terminology

Status: **Garden v15.6 candidate terminology — not part of published Garden v15.5**.

For the Garden v15.6 candidate line, **GAIA** expands to:

> **Garden Artificial Intelligence Architecture**

This expansion applies to **GAIA Titanium** and **GAIA Diamond**.

`Artificial` is intentional: GAIA covers the broader Garden AI architecture, including safety and assurance components that are not themselves autonomous agents. Expanding the acronym as `Garden Autonomous Intelligence Architecture` would narrow the scope incorrectly.

This is a terminology clarification only. It does not change behavior, contracts, authority, HSA, safety/assurance semantics, topology, or DesignEpoch rules.

The machine-readable source-design candidate is `design_deltas/v15.6/DELTASET-2026-09-14-GAIA-NAMING.json`. Published v15.5 remains immutable. The complete v15.6 five-file candidate should propagate this definition only after the normal review/admission gates pass.
