# Garden Main — Design Archive and Candidate Corpus

**Current economic status: the owner has made Garden free for everyone.** See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The owner believes Garden ideas may have very large economic value—potentially billions or trillions if their claimed usefulness proves out. The present state remains fully free. If that free status is ever changed for rights/value that remain controllable, the intended alternative is a Garden Common Fund governed by approximately 100,000 randomly selected, periodically rotating ordinary people through a politically independent and capture-resistant mechanism. No third concentrated-control option is intended.

## v15.10 — major design change

v15.10 moved Garden toward a compact typed/generative abstraction rather than merely adding another module set. It preserves six GSL Pillars, ten Core Objects, twenty-four Core Relations and seven Design Forms; uses cross-cutting facets for inspection; and introduces the GCSC/SAL/SAC direction for bounded semantic-combination coverage, admissibility and candidate artifact generation.

Generated material cannot self-admit, create authority, or replace specialist source meaning. Compact/deterministic expansion must preserve unique equations, assumptions, exceptions, fallbacks and ownership. The later Tree Core work adds explicit owner/dependency/status/source retention requirements.

The repository intentionally does **not** retain the multi-megabyte generated v15.10 catalogue/HTML retention editions merely to preserve hashes. The high-signal design delta and substantive source candidates are retained directly.
