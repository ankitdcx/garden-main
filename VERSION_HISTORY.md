## How to interpret the lineage

- **Whole-release candidate:** a complete proposed successor source set, stored under `canonical/candidates/`.
- **Incremental delta:** a smaller proposal, source extract, research record or recovery item, stored under `design_deltas/`.
- **Historical material:** retained for provenance and comparison; it may contain statements that were accurate only in its original context.
- **Future planning:** an evaluation path, not a release or an implied commitment to create a new canonical version.

A version may have both a complete candidate and supporting deltas. These are distinct artifacts, not interchangeable copies. A candidate can be deferred, incomplete, superseded within its own workstream or useful only as historical evidence.

## Authority rule

Garden v15.5 / GSL v45.1 remains canonical unless a successor is separately reviewed and admitted under the applicable process. A newer date, higher version number, folder name, generated projection, positive review or passing local test does not change that status.
